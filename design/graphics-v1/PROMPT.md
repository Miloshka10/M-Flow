# Встроенная генерация изображения — полный запрос

Режим: новое изображение, `transparent_background=true`. Вложения пользователя
использованы только как примеры неудачной текущей графики, не как редактируемые изображения.

Use case: product-mockup / original editorial illustration. Create one polished, distinctive website hero asset for M-Flow, a school research and project workspace. It is ONLY an illustration asset, not a website mockup. Main subject: a carefully made small architectural truss bridge prototype in ivory card and graphite structural strips, standing on a slightly angled stack of two off-white research sheets; one precise yellow technical pencil rests diagonally beside the sheets, and one small yellow binder clip holds a corner. The paper has only restrained technical construction linework of the bridge, no charts, no statistics, no writing. Concept: real student work becomes a tangible result. Art direction: premium design-magazine still life, tactile thick paper, precise joinery, fine graphite strokes, realistic subtle edge shadows, elegant photographic/material quality, not a cartoon icon, not toy plastic, not generic corporate 3D. Palette tightly limited to off-white, pale grey, graphite #242427, and restrained warm yellow #ffdd2d accents. No greenery, no other colors. Composition: compact coherent still-life, three-quarter overhead view, bridge centered on the papers, full silhouette visible, approximately 4:3 landscape composition. Strong legible silhouette at 300 px wide, moderate detail rather than clutter. All objects are physically resting together; no floating objects, no separate ornamental badges. Transparent alpha background, soft restrained contact shadows only. Leave generous transparent safety margin on every side. Designed to sit on a flat graphite website panel without a rectangular image background and also be usable on pale or yellow backgrounds. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark. Avoid folders, checkmark cards, rockets, spheres, abstract cubes, generic swooshes, colorful gradients, UI fragments, people, fake dashboards, excessive reflection, glows.
